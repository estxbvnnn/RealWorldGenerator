OverworldPlus bundles structure data from four mods by YUNGNICKYOUNG
(https://github.com/YUNG-GANG), each licensed under the GNU Lesser General
Public License v3.0 (LGPL-3.0). Their licence files are in this folder
(LICENSE_Yungs*), together with the GNU GPL v3.0 text the LGPL builds on
(LICENSE_GPL-3.0.txt).

  YUNG's Better Strongholds      26.1.2-Fabric-6.1.1  -> yung/betterstrongholds/
  YUNG's Better Jungle Temples   26.1.2-Fabric-4.1.1  -> yung/betterjungletemples/
  YUNG's Better Witch Huts       26.1.2-Fabric-5.1.1  -> yung/betterwitchhuts/
  YUNG's Better Ocean Monuments  26.1.2-Fabric-5.1.1  -> yung/betteroceanmonuments/

What was taken: the structure templates (structure/*.nbt), template pools,
processor lists and chest loot tables, unmodified — except that the
jungle temples' mod-integration pieces (Create, Supplementaries, Alex's
Mobs) are left out, since they only ever generate with those mods.

What was rebuilt: YUNG's jigsaw placement rules and structure processors,
reimplemented for Paper in com.estxbvnnn.overworldplus.structures.yung
(JigsawLibrary, JigsawAssembler, Processing, YungStructures, Nbt). That
package is derived from the mods' behaviour and is itself offered under
the LGPL-3.0; its source is published with OverworldPlus.
